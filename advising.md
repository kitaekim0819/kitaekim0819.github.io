## Student Advising & Mentorship

I advise and mentor graduate and undergraduate students interested in business analytics, applied machine learning, causal inference, and decision support systems.

### Graduate Thesis Mentorship

- **Spillover Effects of Short-Form Content on Platform Engagement** *(2025)*
  - *Methodology:* Staggered Difference-in-Differences (DiD) & Propensity Score Matching
  - *Focus:* Analyzing creator and viewer dynamics following platform monetization and feature launches.

- **Causal Impact of Same-Day Delivery on Sales and Product Returns** *(2024)*
  - *Methodology:* Difference-in-Differences (DiD) & Matching methods across product categories
  - *Focus:* Quantifying supply chain and fulfillment speed effects on consumer purchasing and return behavior.
