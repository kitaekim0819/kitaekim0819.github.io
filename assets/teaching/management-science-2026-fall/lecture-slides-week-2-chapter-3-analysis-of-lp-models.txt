Samyoung Industrial, a manufacturer of table knives and forks, is determining the production volume for each product to maximize profits using its current production facilities. This product is of excellent quality, so they wish to keep it as a manufactured item. Regarding the green process and the next week in which this product is handled, the available workforce for each process is 700 hours for the press process and 1,000 hours for the scroll process. Producing one bag of handles and knives requires 12 minutes (0.2 hours) of press process manpower and 30 minutes (0.5 hours) of scroll process manpower, while producing one case of forks requires 24 minutes (0.4 hours) of press process manpower and 15 minutes (0.25 hours) of scroll process manpower. Samyoung Industrial intends to select the weapons for handles and forks to maximize total profit over the next week.

Slide 15
FINDING C
Two lines, two equations


1.2X₁ + 4X₂ = 240     and     0.5X₁ + X₂ = 81

From the second:  X₂ = 81 − 0.5X₁

Substitute:  1.2X₁ + 4(81 − 0.5X₁) = 240

−0.8X₁ + 324 = 240   →   X₁ = 105

X₂ = 81 − 0.5(105) = 28.5

Z = 20(105) + 50(28.5) = 3525

THE HALF UNIT
What does 28.5 amplifiers mean?


Half a unit: start it today, finish it tomorrow

If output must be whole numbers, round — then re-check feasibility

X₂ = 28  →  X₁ = 106,  Z = 3520

X₂ = 29  →  X₁ = 103,  Z = 3510

Rounding never beats the LP optimum — whole-number models are integer programming, Chapter 7

SLACK
Slack variables


A “≤” constraint need not use all of its capacity

Slack  =  capacity  −  amount used

S₁ = 240 − (1.2X₁ + 4X₂)          (assembly hours unused)

S₂ = 81 − (0.5X₁ + X₂)          (inspection hours unused)

S₃ = 40 − X₂          (chips unused)

BINDING
Binding constraints — slack is zero


S₁ = 240 − (1.2 × 105 + 4 × 28.5) = 0

S₂ = 81 − (0.5 × 105 + 28.5) = 0

Assembly and inspection are used 100% — both are binding

The optimum sits exactly where these two lines cross

THE INSIGHT
What is holding production back?


Not the chips — 11.5 a day go unused

Assembly and inspection hours are the bottleneck

More hours there would raise profit; more chips would not

Binding constraints are where management should look first
