Additional examples for Chapter 2: 3 Problems



   1. Fund M plans to raise 800 million won from new investors to invest in K Oil and H
      Steel stocks. The expected annual return is 30,000 won per share for K Oil and
      50,000 won per share for H Steel. Currently, the stock price per share is 250,000
      won for K Oil and 500,000 won for H Steel. Fund M assesses the risk index of K
      Oil at 0.5 per share and the risk index of H Steel at 0.25 per share and plans to
      maintain the total risk index within 700. Additionally, as a matter of policy, the
      fund plans not to invest more than 1,000 shares in K Oil. Formulate this problem
      as a linear optimization model.

3. An investment firm is investing 10 billion won across two stocks: Petroleum
   Corporation and Steel Corporation. Based on data analysis for each company's
   stock, they have created the following table.
             Stock               Price per        Expected Annual       Risk index
                                  share           Return per share      per share
                               (KRW/share)         (KRW/share)          (1/share)
    Petroleum Corporation             25,000                   6,000            0.5
      Steel Corporation               50,000                  10,000           0.25

   The risk index per share indicates the risk level of each stock. Petroleum
   Corporation appears to have a higher risk level than its peers. The firm invests
   subject to the constraints that the portfolio’s total risk index does not exceed
   70,000 and that its investment in Petroleum Corporation does not exceed
   100,000 shares. Develop a linear optimization model that maximizes the total
   annual return.
