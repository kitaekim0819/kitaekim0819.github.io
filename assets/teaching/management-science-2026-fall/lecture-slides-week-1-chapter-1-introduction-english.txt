The systematic decision-making process
Every decision follows this shape, whether or not anyone writes it down. Management science makes the middle steps explicit.

         Grasp                  Recognize            Present feasible          Set decision             Evaluate the          Select the best            Act on the

      Information is gathered only once the alternatives are on the table — and both kinds matter. Numbers alone do not set the criteria, and judgement alone
      does not evaluate the options. Management science works on the quantitative half without pretending the other half does not exist.
