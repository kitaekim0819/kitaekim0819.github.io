              X₁ + X₂ ≤ 7         (production capacity)
              X₁ ≤ X₂             (product mix)

Here, X₁ is the number of Type A ships and X₂ is the number of Type B ships.

Problem 5
Pharmaceutical Company A is planning its advertising for next month using magazines and
television. Each one-page magazine advertisement costs KRW 12 million and reaches
approximately 500,000 people. Each TV advertisement costs KRW 30 million and reaches
approximately 1 million people. The advertising budget is KRW 1 billion. Management has
established the following goals, listed in order of priority.

1. Do not exceed the budget.
2. Run no more than 10 TV advertisements.
3. Run at least 5 magazine advertisements.
4. Reach at least 30 million people.

Develop a goal programming model to determine the numbers of magazine and TV
advertisements.
