                      Management Science is a field of study that uses mathematical modeling and scientific analysis
                      techniques to find the most efficient optimal solutions to complex decision-making problems faced
                      by companies or organizations, and applies these solutions to real-world management to support
      Overview
                      rational decision-making. In this course, students will learn management science knowledge and
                      problem-solving skills through various methods including theoretical lectures, problem-solving
                      exercises, and hands-on practice.
                      1) Understanding the necessity of management science and real-world application cases
                      2) Understanding various methodologies used in management science, such as optimization

                      4) Understanding future development directions in management science (research on artificial

IX. Regarding Disabled Students


「Rules Pertaining to Disabled Student Support」 Disabled students are entitled to special conditions or assistance when
taking the class, receiving materials, completing homework assignments and writing exams. Students should consult the
professor, Academic Affairs Team or Student Affairs Team regarding particular needs prior to registering for the class.



IX. Precautions for Using Generative AI



Generative AI may reflect social and cultural biases embedded in its training data and can produce inaccurate or
contextually inappropriate results due to "hallucinations" (factual inaccuracies). Therefore, students must critically
review and verify all AI-generated content using reliable sources. If a student fails to recognize these limitations and
submits work containing AI-generated errors, the student bears full responsibility for any resulting academic penalties.
