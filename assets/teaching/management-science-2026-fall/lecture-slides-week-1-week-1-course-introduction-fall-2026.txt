WHY MANAGEMENT SCIENCE


How do organizations find the best decision
among countless possible options?
Management science uses mathematical modeling and scientific analysis to find optimal solutions to complex decision-making
problems, then applies them to real-world management to support rational decisions.

G E N E R AT I V E A I P O L I C Y


Generative AI is permitted — conditionally

        ✓       Permitted                                                                       ✕     Not permitted
                •      Brainstorming ideas and drafting outlines                                      •     Submitting AI output as your own work
                •      Sentence correction and grammar checking                                       •     Using AI without disclosing that you did
                •      Explaining concepts, generating draft code                                     •     Any use during exams and quizzes




      The final work must be produced by you, and any use of AI requires a Generative AI Usage Statement.
      The statement records the tool used, the purpose and scope of use, your main prompts, and your own contribution and revisions, followed by a self-check
      pledge. Its content is not directly reflected in grading — it exists to ensure transparency.




      Generative AI reflects social and cultural bias and can produce inaccurate results. If you submit AI-generated errors, the academic responsibility is yours.
