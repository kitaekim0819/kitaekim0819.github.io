## Teaching Interests

My teaching interests lie at the intersection of business analytics, operations research, and artificial intelligence, with a focus on preparing students to solve real-world business problems using quantitative methods.

- **Business Analytics & Data Science** (Machine Learning, Business Statistics, Data Mining)
- **Applied Artificial Intelligence** (AI/ML Applications in Business and Marketing)
- **Decision Support Systems & Optimization** (Mathematical Programming, Management Science)
- **Management Information Systems (MIS)** (Digital Platforms, IT Strategy)

## Teaching Philosophy

My goal as an educator is to equip students with both the rigorous theoretical foundations of data science and the practical, hands-on programming and modeling skills needed to solve complex business problems. I emphasize active learning, data-driven decision-making, and the application of optimization frameworks to real-world business scenarios.
